About the model

How a tweet becomes a prediction, what the model learned from and where it falls short.

How it works

1

Clean the text

Text is lower-cased, links and symbols are removed and common filler words are dropped. Negations like “not” are kept.

2

Turn words into numbers

A TF-IDF vectorizer (single words and word pairs) converts the text into features a model can read.

3

Predict

An XGBoost classifier estimates a probability for each category. The highest score is shown as the result.

Limitations

✓   Not a diagnosis. The model detects patterns in language. Only a qualified professional can assess mental health.

✓   Imbalanced data. Some categories have far fewer examples, so predictions for them may be less reliable.

✓   Short or sarcastic text can be misread. Longer, clearer text works best.

✓   Confidence matters. A low percentage means the model is unsure. Treat results with extra caution.

✓   Privacy. Recent analysis history is stored in your browser, not on the server.
