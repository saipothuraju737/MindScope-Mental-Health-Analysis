How it works

From raw tweet to prediction in three steps.

01

You write a tweet

The text is lower-cased, links and special characters are removed, and common filler words are dropped.

02

Words become numbers

A TF-IDF vectorizer (unigrams + bigrams) turns the text into features the model can understand.

03

The model predicts

An XGBoost classifier returns a probability for every category. The highest one is shown as the result.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a diagnosis?

No. The model only finds patterns in language based on its training data. It cannot diagnose anyone. Only a qualified professional can assess mental health.

How accurate is it?

It works best on longer, clearly worded text. Very short or sarcastic tweets can be misread. The confidence percentage shows how sure the model is — low confidence means treat the result with extra caution.

Is my text saved?

Text is processed by the app to make the prediction and is not written to a database. The history list lives only in your own browser and you can clear it any time.

Why are some results "Normal" or "Depression" so often?

Those categories have the most training samples, so the model has seen them most. Rarer categories like Personality disorder have far fewer examples.
